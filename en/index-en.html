Boundless Magic

Video game | Team Project January 2026 - present

Description

Boundless Magic is a 2D side-scrolling endless runner for mobile, where the player evades obstacles, fights enemies and tries to escape from the dungeon and get the highest score. The player can cast multiple powerful spells, that aid him in various ways during the run.

Spells behave like combos. To cast a spell, the player needs to kill certain enemy types on their way e.g. two red enemies and one blue, but killing enemies in sequence is not required.

Project Ideogram

Video game | Prototype March 2025 - present

Description

Project Ideogram (prototype name) is a 2D puzzle adventure game with hand-drawn animations. The core of the gameplay is the translation of words, concepts and sentences from natural language into ideogram writing I created. The player must also combine already known ideograms with each other to create new ideograms and progress to the next levels.

Solving puzzles will be enriched with a story about the life of an ancient man, full of intricacies, which will be presented on the model of cave drawings. The animations will be hand-drawn in stop-motion, which will give a distinctive artistic style. The storyline is expected to help the player with the puzzles and sometimes guide him to the right solution, as well as generally enrich the whole experience.

Warsaw Merchants

Board game | Video game June 2023

Description

Warsaw Merchants is an economic board game for 2-4 people. The players' goal is to buy goods at the lowest possible price and sell at the highest possible price. Players must adjust to changing demand and commodity prices, choose their strategy accordingly, and sometimes rely on a little luck.
