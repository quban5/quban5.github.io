My mindset

What I appreciate most about professional work is when I can design and create something that will be useful to others. Whether it's an intuitive and functional interface, or a graphic, poster, or brochure that will not only be visually appealing but also provide the necessary information.

Learning new things has always given me a great sense of satisfaction. One of my bigger projects was a website for the touristic NGO Oddział PTTK “Szaniec” w Skierniewicach, for which (and for this portfolio) I learned HTML, CSS, and a little bit of JavaScript. Wanting to create my own modded Minecraft server, I learned the basics of Linux and how to set up a server on Oracle Cloud, as well as how to manage it via SSH and FTP. So it doesn't matter to me if there's something I don't know or can't do if I'm really interested in something, because I'll try to learn it right away.

Lately, I've been trying to fix the drive on my good old Xbox 360, where pretty much everything that could break has broken. I've gone from learning how optical drives work all the way to soldering and distinguishing different types of lubricants. I'm not giving up and I'm still working on it because I feel like I'm almost there. That's the kind of attitude I try to have in every field I engage in.


What do I do in my studies?

I am currently studying Audiovisual Media and Digital Culture (Master's degree) at the University of Łódź. Through my bachelor's and master's degree courses, I have gained theoretical knowledge in areas including media studies, game studies, cultural studies and film studies. I have taken courses in: graphic design, UI and UX design, new media art, databases, information visualisation, generative art, art history and culture theory.

The topic of my thesis is the problem of the availability of older games in digital distribution. Among other things, I intend to present this phenomenon and its complexities, and, with the help of Python scripts, collect and analyze data from Metacritic and investigate what percentage of PC games released by 2015 are not legally available for purchase in digital stores (not second-hand).


Outside of work

Video games hold a special place in my heart and are my greatest passion. When, at the age of six, I got my hands on Asterix and Obelix XXL and Tigger's Honey Hunt, it became clear to me that I couldn't imagine life without video games.

In my spare time, I love playing all sorts of games. Recently, I've been playing with friends on a modded Minecraft server, as well as Hades II and Hollow Knight: Silksong. Apart from those mentioned above, the series that mean the most to me are: Red Dead Redemption, The Witcher, Portal, Half-Life and Hollow Knight; as well as: Pentiment, Journey and What Remains of Edith Finch and Dark Souls III.

I am also interested in new technology, computer science, pop culture, history and politics. In the summer, on the other hand, I'm into winemaking, and I look after my bonsai trees all year round.
